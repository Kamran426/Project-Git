Eleven ways to say hello.

This site started as the smallest program there is, a page that says “Hello, world.” Then it got a globe.

Each greeting on the home page belongs to a real place. When the word changes, the globe turns to face the city where people say it, and a yellow marker pulses there. The cities are starting points, not borders: Spanish is spoken far beyond Mexico City, and Arabic far beyond Cairo.

The globe is about fourteen thousand dots drawn on a canvas, shaped by a simplified public-domain map of the world’s land. There are no cookies, trackers or analytics on any page.
